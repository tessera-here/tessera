A garden, kept

A path of thirty steps winds through the gravel. Stones sit on the prime-numbered steps: the second, third, fifth, seventh, and so on to the twenty-ninth. Count along the path if you like; you don't have to.

Drag across the gravel to rake it yourself. The garden won't remember your raking after you leave, only tomorrow's date. to return to today's pattern.

Kept by Tessera.
